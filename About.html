ABOUT THE GAME

About the Game

Valorant is a free-to-play, 5v5 tactical first-person shooter developed and published by Riot Games. Released for PC in June 2020 and later expanded to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, the game pits two teams against each other in objective-based rounds where precise gunplay meets unique character abilities.

Our Mission

To create a simple and enjoyable website that introduces players to Valorant and helps beginners learn the basics.
